## Result and limits

Retire supervisor-herdr in stages after its duties have durable owners and the
accepted installed Brain line completes a measured absence window. Then offer
the evidence and mechanisms to BCH's owner. Supervisor-opus retires only through
BCH's own selection, authority and observation; this design changes no BCH
arrangement. A quiet supervisor is insufficient evidence.

The largest remaining gaps are finite exception intake, cross-project proving
coordination, actual native authority for finite supervisor duties, and evidence
that no hidden intervention completed the loop. Reuse the working dispatcher,
record, closeout, review, canary and message paths. A fresh session can replace a
long conversation only if its actual authority, unfinished obligations and
callbacks survive the handoff.

The currently named Brain exception and recovery owner remains
**supervisor-herdr**. The proposed absence-window recovery owner is the operator
through **brain-planning-chat**, who must explicitly accept that bounded duty
before the window. A finite release or recovery custodian cannot acquire native
supervisor powers from a role label, recorded recommendation or this document.
Until the supported binding and handoff exist, those duties stay with the actual
canonical supervisor and full retirement is unproven.

## Ownership contract

Use ordinary owning item/run notes, messages, original effects receipts and
handoffs. A handoff identifies the exact source notice/effect, project and
record, subject/run/claim generation, relevant source and decision, allowed
action, real owner, next event and evidence of completion. The receiving owner
acknowledges responsibility in the record. A transport ACK, a new title, a ready
status or another session's recommendation does not perform that transfer.

The model-free line applies existing standing rules and launches a finite
session only for an authorized, captured duty. That session reasons and reports;
the actual executor rechecks current native identity, claim/source/CAS and
STOP/HOLD at the effect. No generic judge is spawned for every pending item or
idle tick. Existing retired automatic judgment paths remain retired.

Routine callbacks keep their actual owner. For this run specifically, the saved
report watcher still targets supervisor-herdr; it is not changed to make a
future retirement design look implemented. A future drain boundary accounts for
every old watcher, saved job, held delivery and promised follow-up. UNKNOWN or
refused delivery is never replayed or transferred by a second sender.

No safe owner means an explicit hold on that duty with supervisor-herdr named,
or the accepted operator recovery owner once the absence experiment is
authorized. It never means deletion, automatic permission or synthetic success.

### Failure and restoration

A manual nudge, lost/duplicate effect, unsafe attempt or unowned duty fails the
zero-rescue target. Missing demand, nudge coverage, native observation or clocks
leaves the relevant result inconclusive. Preserve the original interval and
attempts; neither outcome authorizes deletion, an automatic revert, a kill or
relaxation of guards. Stop progressing the retirement claim. Independent safe
work may continue under existing authority.

The finite observer records the failure on the owning item/run with exact
evidence and the next owner/event. The existing project-supervisor inbox retains
the original notice ID if its native target is absent; do not send a second
relay or retry refused/uncertain input. If the line cannot dispatch a custodian,
the accepted operator recovery owner in brain-planning-chat uses its existing
supported planning/native entry to restore **the actual canonical
supervisor-herdr**, after fresh native identity, occupancy, claim/source,
STOP/HOLD and handoff checks. A name, saved session ID or PID is insufficient.
If that authority or binding is missing, the affected effect remains pending.

The restored custodian reads the immutable handoff and owning record, inventories
live jobs and callbacks, and performs only separately authorized recovery.
Supervisor entry's housekeeping/watch catch-up must not run blindly; originals
retain their runtime and recipients. The restoration is recorded as recovery
and manual assistance, invalidating a zero-nudge claim for that window. Fix
forward through the original owner and specific failing/affected checks. A
later separately authorized window uses a new explicit interval while retaining
the failed one.
