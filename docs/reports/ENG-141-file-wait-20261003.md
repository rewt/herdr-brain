This demonstrates the observable delivery hazard on this host; it does not establish the precise internal FSEvents startup mechanism. The deterministic silent-watcher controls do not depend on that mechanism or on host scheduling.

The helper now independently reselects parents and rereads the predicate on a 10, 20, 40, 80, 160, then 250 ms backoff. It retains one recovery timer and one deadline timer. The deadline is scaled once using measured load, and performs a final evidence check before rejecting so delayed event-loop scheduling does not reject already-published evidence. Completion, cancellation and failures clear the timers and close watchers. Notifications still require predicate-confirmed evidence; the misleading-event/cancellation regression verifies that incomplete content cannot succeed. Renamed the parent-directory test to remove its obsolete “without polling” claim.

`nativeOwner` keeps content-addressed compilation reuse and process-exit cache cleanup. The new PTY regression checks every environment key after both the initial compilation/launch and a cached launch, with `HERDR_TEST_OFFLINE_ROOT` explicitly absent inside the child.
