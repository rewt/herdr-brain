The new events are `lesson.proposed`, `lesson.activated`, `lesson.retired` and
`lesson.superseded`. They use the existing transaction, actor attribution and
append-only store. No event or folded lesson stores status. The read projection
adds the derived state. Claims normalize to one line and permit 1–400 Unicode
code points. Evidence requires at least one existing item, run or `event:N`;
all references and supersession constraints are checked under the log lock.

`record lesson add` proposes by default. `record lesson activate L-n` activates
proposed or retired lessons; repeatable `--supersedes L-old` atomically replaces
older lessons. `record lesson retire L-n --reason TEXT` retains why a claim stopped
helping. Superseded identities remain permanent evidence and cannot be resurrected
or assigned another replacement. Unchanged mutations are idempotent.

`record lessons` defaults to active lessons. `--state` also accepts proposed,
retired, superseded and all; `--scope` selects project, fleet or framework.
`--path` matches normalized literal paths at directory boundaries; `--module`
matches exact names. Matching either supplied selector is sufficient, and lessons
with no selectors remain broadly applicable. Exact matches lead directory
prefixes, then broad lessons; specificity, scope and numeric ID provide stable
ordering. Text bounds claim, target, proposer, evidence and retirement summaries;
JSON preserves complete facts and evidence.
