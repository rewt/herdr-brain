# Folder closeout and parked-wait reconciliation

ENG-931 / R-1254 owns this source correction. The operator's closeout-only focus
includes the old backlog; it does not waive native identity, preservation,
UNKNOWN/refusal, STOP/HOLD or original-owner boundaries.

The paused line's record contains repeated `removal needs 69 s; pass has 7 s`
and deadline keeps. Its finite role capped every turn at 45 seconds, while the
guarded remover correctly requires an inventory-sized allowance starting at
30 seconds, plus a separate branch allowance. The line now supplies the existing
three-minute closer allowance, independently of the general-pass budget.
Ingestion retains its 15-second sub-budget. Exit is deferred before its intent
if the remaining turn cannot cover the original exit observation.

Fairness now spans direct report targets and legacy physical continuations.
Selection skips legacy bindings that the guarded executor cannot admit and
interrupted Git commands whose original result is unknown; those retain their
typed exceptions instead of spending every turn on empty drains.
Original-binding refresh has a finite 15-second allowance, including priority
every fourth turn, so continuously eligible direct keeps cannot starve it.
Neither scheduling change grants effect authority; the existing executor
rechecks original source/allocation/claim, native session/process, Git identity,
expected head, preservation, STOP/HOLD and current service ownership.

Discarded duplicate preservation bundles now have an immutable effect row.
Recovery ignores discarded or absent bundles and does not mistake a reset's
manual removal for a proven automatic removal. A successful original native
tab close is recorded before receipt pruning. Native-return failures retain
their original reason/code alongside the UNKNOWN obligation, instead of losing
the useful diagnostic behind the generic pending reason.

Each finite turn also reconciles up to 64 original parked waits into
`closeout.wait-classified` rows. They retain exact wait/source/evaluation
references, independent resource receipts, first-seen age, actual owner and
continuation. Ordinary pre-effect conditions can use the guarded next turn;
uncertain invocations, refusals, changed allocation generations, missing native
identity and unproven removals remain explicit held exceptions. Original events,
queue files and failed native attempts stay immutable. A classification is never
an effect completion or an alternate cleanup allocation. The general diagnostic
reports classified retained waits separately from completed and unknown waits.

Open unreported runs receive an `active-unreported` allocation disposition with
their original dispatch journal and claim generation. A saved original dispatch
error receives `dispatch-error-unreported`, retaining its journal hash, error,
failure code, stage and binding status. The remaining ARCH-57 allocation has an
original watcher-start error and no saved SID; it cannot satisfy the supported
original-producer abandonment proof. An idle pane and elapsed age are not abandonment.
The dirty validation-contract folder must retain its actual unsaved bytes until
its owner preserves or commits them. Original owners must supply missing
completion/never-started evidence or finalize their own work; this correction
does not fabricate those facts or signal processes merely from their cwd.
