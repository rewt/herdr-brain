# Resolve the observed failure

Recover the assigned ask, checkable done condition, current failure, owning
project/claim and original command/receipt. List the paths and effects the task
excludes. Separate a product defect from runner/environment failure, stale source,
missing evidence and missing authority; do not make an error disappear by weakening
the protected property or widening permissions.

Choose the smallest observation that distinguishes the leading explanations.
Reuse the designated producer's existing evidence rather than rerunning a whole
suite. For a code correction, preserve the original failure and use the project's
focused runner once for failing, affected or unrun controls on the fixed candidate.
Keep source, command, exit and log attached to the owning report. Unrun is not PASS.

After two or three failed attempts on the same premise, change the explanation or
approach and record why. This is a reframe signal, not an elapsed-time stop or
permission to abandon independent work. If the new frame needs a contract or
authority decision, take that specific question with a recommendation to the
recorded PROJECT SUPERVISOR. Do not add a relay chain or ask the operator whether
routine engineering should continue.

A native harness refusal stops the refused effect immediately. Preserve the
original result; never retry through another command shape, executor or identity.
Inspect uncertain starts, deliveries and review attempts through their original
saved receipts/session paths. Do not replace UNKNOWN work, forge bindings, restart
services/watchers, edit saved runtimes/pins or use supervisor cycle mutations as
a worker repair shortcut. A runtime test's EPERM is diagnostic evidence, not
automatic authority to change permissions.

Fix only what blocks the assigned outcome within scope. Record unrelated useful
discoveries as proposals awaiting selection; more than three ordinary follow-ups
share one reshape note retaining the sources. Keep formal findings linked to
their actual correction obligations. No extra full review chain follows a fix.
Majors need specific correction evidence before final release.

When the outcome is met, self-land owned finished source through the project's
guarded entry and return its report through the existing watcher/record path.
If blocked, report the exact remaining condition and retained source/evidence.
Use natural-language operator envelopes in the owning record; push only an actual
pending explicitly reserved human question through its existing path.
